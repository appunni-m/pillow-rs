# Benchmarks

Use the [benchmark results site](https://appunni-m.github.io/pillow-rs/benchmarks/)
for per-workload latency, sample counts, spread, correctness, and backend
receipts. Every snapshot identifies its source revision and measurement date.

Read the [benchmark protocol](docs/BENCHMARKING.md) before comparing results.
Documentation builds render recorded evidence; they do not rerun benchmarks or
turn historical measurements into a current-release performance claim.
